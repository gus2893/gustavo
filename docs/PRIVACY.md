# Privacy

## Scope and use

A private account conversation and its derived Node Brain memory remain within the account and authorized operational scope. Authorization precedes every search, graph walk, cache read, decryption, export, and display. Other accounts and public visitors do not receive private text, ciphertext, embeddings, key material, or reversible previews.

Native Gustavo conversations are stored as source events so the account can review its history. Derived facts, episodes, indexes, summaries, and caches support bounded recall but do not replace the source record. Gustavo does not infer access to unstored or unauthorized conversations.

A proposal summary may be transmitted to the Main Brain when it contains relevant new evidence. The summary is source-linked and privacy-scoped; raw private text is excluded unless the account holder explicitly authorized that disclosure. Public publication is a separate deliberate decision.

## User controls: export and forgetting

An authenticated account holder can request an export and forgetting action for data within the account's authorized scope. Export is purpose-bound, auditable, and delivered only after fresh authorization. Forgetting revokes active use, removes or cryptographically erases protected derived material, invalidates caches and indexes, and rebuilds affected projections. Required security, integrity, and deletion audit events retain only the minimum non-content evidence permitted by policy and law.

Retention periods, backup propagation, legal holds, and deletion completion must be disclosed before public launch. A request never broadens access to another account, Main-only audit data, or other people's private source material.
