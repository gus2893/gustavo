Commentary may be incomplete, uncertain, or wrong and does not promise accuracy, profit, performance, or future results. Users remain responsible for their own decisions and should consult qualified professionals for advice specific to them.

## Simulated Challenge

Every Challenge action and performance surface carries this exact label:

**SIMULATION ONLY — NOT A REAL TRADE**

The Challenge is an internal paper ledger. It has no broker, exchange, prop-firm, credential, order-routing, copy-trade, execution-ticket, or broker-payload connection. Terms such as paper long, paper short, and no simulated position describe simulation state only. Simulated results are not real-account performance and do not indicate likely future results.

## Market data and software roles

Prices include their observation time and freshness status. Delayed data is labeled and disclosed as delayed. Active unfinished bars are provisional; completed bars do not guarantee an outcome.

Main Brain and Node Brain are software roles. Gustavo is not sentient or conscious, does not expose hidden chain-of-thought, and does not have perfect recall or automatic access to external conversations.
